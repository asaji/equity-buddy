Twitter Authentication

RECOMMENDED

Export cookies from your browser

This is the most reliable method. Twitter's login flow often blocks automated sign-ins.

  1. On your Mac or PC, open Chrome or Firefox and log into x.com
  2. Install the Cookie-Editor browser extension
  3. Click the Cookie-Editor icon → Export → Export as JSON (copies to clipboard)
  4. SSH into Unraid and run:
    nano /mnt/user/appdata/equitybuddy/cookies/twitter_cookies.json Paste the clipboard contents, save with Ctrl+O then Ctrl+X
  5. Restart the worker:
    docker restart equitybuddy-worker

Cookies are stored at /mnt/user/appdata/equitybuddy/cookies/twitter_cookies.json on the host. Re-export if scraping stops working after a few weeks.

Username / password login

Fallback option — may fail if Twitter challenges the login with a captcha or 2FA.
